# Ignite-Yoga-Studio-Recruitment-Process-Optimization
Operational Business Analysis for a hybrid Yoga startup, Ignite Yoga Studio. Features end-to-end recruitment process modeling, stakeholder mapping, and Root Cause Analysis (5-Whys) to optimize talent acquisition and accelerate revenue generation.

## 📌 Project Overview
As the owner/BA of a new startup, Ignite Yoga Studio, I conducted a Business Analysis to build a scalable recruitment process for a hybrid (physical and online) yoga business. 
The project focused on transitioning the studio from the foundational setup phase to active revenue generation by securing high-quality, certified talent.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
1. **Modeling:** Lucidchart (Process Flow & Root Cause Analysis).
2. **Techniques:** Requirement Elicitation, Stakeholder Management, 5-Whys Analysis.
3. **Strategy:** Hybrid Business Modeling (Boutique Studio + Scalable Online Platform).

## 🔍 Key Findings (Root Cause Analysis)
Through a "5-Whys" analysis, I identified that the primary bottleneck in teacher acquisition was a **single-resource HR constraint**, leading to delayed hiring cycles despite high candidate volume.

## 💡 Proposed Improvements
1. **Specific Auditions:** Implementing a "Practice Teaching" round for specialized roles (Athlete Coach vs. Online Expert).
2. **Digital Portfolio Reviews:** Shortlisting based on digital teaching clips to verify methodologies before the physical interview.
3. **Enhanced Communication:** Automating structured email responses to ensure candidate transparency and professional branding.
